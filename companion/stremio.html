streams

streaming server

Stremio's own streaming server, for torrent streams and addons that need proxied headers. The TV fetches from it directly. Leave empty if you only use direct-link addons.

AirPlay away from home

For AirPlay to a TV that isn't on your home Wi-Fi, like a friend's or a hotel's. The server's public address (Tailscale Funnel, see resolver/README.md). While AirPlay is on, the receiver page opened over https:// sends that TV a link that plays only that video and stops working after 8 hours. Leave empty to turn this off.

Plus18

Plus18 has its own addons, catalogs and library. Normal mode hides 18+ titles everywhere, including search results and your library. Switch with the 18+ button at the top.

playback

Choosing a stream casts it to the TV and keeps it in your library. Use save on a stream to download it without casting. The TV remembers where you stopped and continues with the next saved episode.
